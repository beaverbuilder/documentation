Learn how to download, install, and activate Beaver Builder AI on your WordPress site.

## Download

Download the Beaver Builder AI plugin zip file from your account.

1. Go to the [Beaver Builder website](https://www.wpbeaverbuilder.com/) and click **My Account**, then sign in with your Beaver Builder username and password.
2. From your account dashboard, click the **Downloads** tab to see the plugins included with your subscription.
3. Find **Beaver Builder AI** in the list and click the download link to save the plugin zip file to your computer.

:::tip
Before continuing, review the [system requirements](requirements.md) to make sure your site is ready for Beaver Builder AI.
:::

## Install

Beaver Builder AI installs like any other WordPress plugin.

1. From your WordPress admin dashboard, go to **Plugins > Add New Plugin**.
2. Click **Upload Plugin**, then select the Beaver Builder AI zip file that you downloaded from your account.
3. Click **Install Now** and wait for WordPress to upload and install the plugin.
4. After installation is complete, click **Activate Plugin**.

## After Installation

After activating Beaver Builder AI, two new areas become available in the WordPress admin:

- **Design Systems** – Appears under the **Beaver Builder** menu. If Beaver Builder is not active, Design Systems appears as its own top-level menu.
- **Beaver Builder AI settings** – Appears under **Settings > Beaver Builder AI**.

The Beaver Builder AI settings page opens on the **Welcome** tab. Its **Getting Started** section holds four short video walkthroughs (a product overview, the built-in chat, MCP connections, and design kits), alongside the product roadmap and answers to common questions.
